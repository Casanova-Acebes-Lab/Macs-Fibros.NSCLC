Figure 6 human cohort input (not distributed)

R/13_Figure6.R expects the file:
  data/Figure6/firmas_fibros_en_frescos_16abr26_v3.csv

This file contains per-patient signature scores and clinical annotation of the
NSCLC cohort from Hospital Universitario 12 de Octubre (Madrid). These data are
part of an unpublished study and are not distributed in this repository.
Requests should be addressed to the investigators at Hospital Universitario
12 de Octubre through the corresponding author (M. Casanova-Acebes, CNIO).
